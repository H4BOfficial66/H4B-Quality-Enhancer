TikTok Video Export Settings: A Practical Guide

Good upload quality starts with a clean source file and a sensible export. No single setting can guarantee that TikTok will show every upload at its original quality, because the platform may process uploaded videos again.

Important: H4B prepares supported video containers in your browser. It is not an AI upscaler, it does not create missing detail or extra frames, and it cannot guarantee that TikTok will skip its own compression.

1. Choose a suitable video size

For a full-screen vertical post, a 9:16 canvas is a common editing format. A 1080 Ã— 1920 export is a practical target when your original footage has enough detail to support it. If your source was recorded at a lower resolution, exporting a larger frame does not restore detail that was never captured. Avoid stretching the video; crop carefully and keep important subjects inside the visible frame.

2. Keep the frame rate close to the source

Start with the frame rate used when recording or editing the footage. If the original clip is 30 frames per second, exporting it at 60 or 120 frames per second does not automatically make motion smoother. A higher frame rate requires genuine extra frames, usually from recording at that rate or using frame interpolation. Unnecessary frame-rate conversion can create duplicate frames or uneven motion.

3. Use a widely compatible format

Practical compatibility choices

  • Container: MP4 is widely supported by browsers and editing tools.
  • Video codec: H.264 is a common compatibility choice.
  • Audio: AAC is commonly supported for encoded video audio.
  • Orientation: Keep the intended vertical or horizontal layout and check that rotation is correct.

Always check the export options in your own editor. Codec support and available presets vary between devices and applications.

4. Avoid unnecessarily extreme bitrates

Higher bitrate can preserve more source detail, but it also creates larger files and does not prevent a social platform from re-encoding an upload. Use your editor's high-quality preset or a reasonable bitrate for the chosen resolution and frame rate. Compare a short test export before rendering a long project. If the file is already clean, repeatedly exporting it through different apps can introduce additional quality loss.

5. Check the file before uploading

  1. Watch the exported file locally from beginning to end.
  2. Check focus, motion, audio sync, orientation and the first and last frames.
  3. Keep an untouched copy of the original export.
  4. Use the H4B tool only with a supported file type, then review the downloaded output.
  5. Upload the reviewed file and check the published result on another device or connection.

What H4B does and does not do

H4B's browser workflow processes supported video containers locally on your device. Container or metadata processing is different from AI enhancement, denoising, sharpening, upscaling or frame generation. It does not guarantee a particular TikTok resolution, bitrate, reach, or final playback quality. TikTok can apply its own processing after upload, so keep the original export and compare results.

Frequently asked questions

Does exporting at 4K guarantee 4K playback on TikTok?

No. A 4K source can contain more detail, but the final playback resolution is controlled by the platform and playback conditions. Do not upscale a low-resolution source only to claim a higher quality.

Should I change a 30 FPS video to 120 FPS?

Usually, keep the frame rate aligned with the source. A metadata change alone cannot create real intermediate frames.

Does H4B bypass TikTok compression?

No bypass is guaranteed. H4B can process supported containers locally, while TikTok may still re-encode the uploaded file.

Are my videos uploaded to H4B servers?

The H4B browser workflow is designed to process selected files locally in the browser. Check the browser network activity and the site's current privacy information if you need to independently audit data handling.
